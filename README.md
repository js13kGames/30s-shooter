# 30s_shooter

A fast-paced and exhilarating shooting game where each stage lasts just 30 seconds.

## 🎮 Game Overview
* **Stages**: 10 stages in total
* **Time Limit**: 30 seconds per stage
* **Player Character**: Unicorn 🦄

## ✨ Key Features
### 🛒 Upgrade Shop
Spend points earned between stages to freely power up your unicorn.

### 🌈 Special Ability: RAINBOW WAVE
By upgrading your ship in the shop, you can unlock the powerful **"RAINBOW WAVE"** skill to wipe out and erase all enemy bullets!

## 🚀 How to Play
1. Open `index.html` in your web browser.
2. Defeat enemies and clear each stage within the 30-second time limit.
3. Power up your unicorn at the shop between stages and try to beat all 10 stages!
