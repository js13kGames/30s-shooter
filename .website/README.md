A fast-paced 30-second shooting game with 10 stages!

Control a magical unicorn and blast through enemies. Your ship fires automatically, so all you need to do is move left and right—making it super easy to play on mobile devices!

Earn coins to power up in the Upgrade Shop. Don't forget to unlock the RAINBOW WAVE to destroy incoming enemy bullets!

Controls & Features

Stages: 10 stages (30 seconds each)

Controls: Move Left/Right (Auto-shoot / Mobile friendly)

Upgrades: Upgrade Shop available (Unlocks RAINBOW WAVE to cancel enemy bullets)
